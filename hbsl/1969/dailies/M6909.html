Pitchers of the Month

It's a Hamsterdam sweep for the top 3 spots with Dierker, Hands and Horlen placing 1-2-3 in runs saved. Larry Dierker pitched 47 innings, allowing 7 runs, 6 earned, on 28 hits and 6 walks, striking out 30 and saved 14.4 runs. Bill Hands saved 10.4 runs and Joe Horlen saved 9.4 runs. Rounding out the top 5 were St. Paul's Jim Maloney and Sophia'sDave McNally.

The dominant relievers of the month were C Street's Steve Hamilton, who saved 5.1 runs above average over 20 innings with a 0.90 ERA, followed by St. Paul's Ken Tatum who saved 5.6 runs in 16 innings with a 0.56 ERA.

The worst pitcher of the month was once again Okinawa's Marty Pattin who cost his team 16.1 runs with 15 innings of 12.60 ERA ball. Also pretty awful were Ringling's Lee Stange (-15.6), the Apostles Lew Krausse (-14.2), Hawaii's Bucky Brandon (-11.5) and Rockford's Denny McLain (-10.9).
