Hot Buttered Soul League
Monthly Awards for April 1969


Good Team of the Month:
Both the ST. PAUL PEEPS (Thom Henninger) and ROCKFORD FILES (Andrew
Bernstein) went 16-6 for the month.  Rockford had the larger run 
margin (65 to 41), and easily led the league in scoring, but due to 
their outstanding pitching and defense, St. Paul had a better 
pythagorean winning percentage (.750 to .731).

Bad Team of the Month:
The CAPE CANAVERAL APOLLOS (Will Carroll) went 6-16, scoring 83 runs
and allowing 117 for a -34 run margin and a .335 Pythagorean winning
percentage.  The 7-15 Quincy Medical Examiners (Mike Canter) had the 
worst run margin (-59) while the 8-14 Apostles of St. Paul (EnglishMess)
with a 70/127 runs scored/allowed had the poorest Pythag of .233.

Hitter of the Month:
The Rockford Files have three of the Top 5 in runs created and Andersonville 
had the #3 and #4 hitters.  The Top 5 are Frank Howard (34.8 RC), Willie
McCovey (33.9), Mike Epstein (27.6), Bobby Bonds (25.5) and Mack Jones (24.6).

Bad Hitter of the Month:
St Paul's Curt Flood put up a .379 OPS for the month batting 115/182/197.
Apostles' Glenn Beckert also had a rough time of it, batting 167/214/179.

Starter of the Month:
Four starting pitchers were within 0.4 runs saved of each other.  Leading 
the group was Northfield's Bill Singer (12.4), Cape Canaveral's Bob Gibson
(12.1), St. Paul's Fritz Peterson (12.1) and St. Paul's Dick Bosman (12.0)

Reliever of the Month:
Rockford's Ron Perranoski and Cary's Eddie Watt each saved 4.6 runs above
average.  Rounding out the group were Rockford's Jack Aker (4.4) and
Northfield's Pete Mikkelsen (4.2).

Bad Pitcher of the Month:
Sanford And Son's Stan Bahnsen allowed 13.2 runs above average in 25.0 innings.
Not far behind was Quincy's Ron Bryant who was 12.1 runs worse than average in
12 innings.
