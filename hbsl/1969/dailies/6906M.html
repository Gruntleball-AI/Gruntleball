Pitchers of the Month

Sophia's Phil Niekro saved 11.4 runs above average for the month of June, pitching 51 innings over 7 games with a 2.12 ERA. With 11.1 runs saved, Northfield's Mike Cuellar was more dominant on a per-inning basis but threw only 39.2 innings, albeit with a 0.68 ERA. Rounding out the field were Cary's Claude Osteen (10.8), Sophia's Don Sutton (10.3) and St. Paul's Jim Palmer (10.1).

The dominant relievers of the month were Cape Canaveral's Clay Carroll, who saved 8.0 runs above average over 32.1 innings, followed by Sophia's Jim McAndrew who saved 6.5 runs in 21 innings with a 0.43 ERA.

The worst pitchers of the month were Hawaii's Tom Burgmeier who cost his team 14.4 runs with 16.1 innings of 10.47 ERA ball. Also pretty awful were Ringling's Jack Billingham (-11.4), Okinawa's Jim Hunter (-11.0) and Sanford's Stan Bahnsen (-10.1).
