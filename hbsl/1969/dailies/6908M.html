Hitters of the Month

Rockford's Frank Howard created 38.8 runs with a 1.189 OPS and 13 homeruns. Howard had 128 plate appearances with a batting line of 381/445/743. Hawaii's Brooks Robinson had the poorest month among regulars, batting 169/194/180 in 93 plate appearances.

Pitchers of the Month

St. Paul's Gary Gentry eeked out the award with just over 13.4 runs saved for the month. Northfield's Juan Marichal was just under 13.4 runs saved. Also noteworthy were Northfield's Mike Cuellar (13.2), Sophia's Dave McNally (13.1), Cape Canaveral's Bob Gibson (12.2), Hamsterdam's Larry Dierker (11.9) and Cary's Blue Moon Odom (11.9).

The dominant relievers of the month were Ringling's Dick Drago, who saved 6.8 runs above average over 20 innings with a 0.90 ERA, followed by St. Paul's Ken Tatum who saved 5.6 runs in 18.1 innings with a 2.45 ERA.

The worst pitchers of the month were Okinawa's Marty Pattin who cost his team 19.7 runs with 11.2 innings of 15.43 ERA ball. Also pretty awful was Cape Canaveral's Gary Peters (-19.3).
