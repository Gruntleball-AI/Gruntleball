Hot Buttered Soul League
Monthly Awards for May 1969


Good Team of the Month:
The CARY HAIRIES (Bryan Smith/Mike Theodore) went 19-9 for the month,
scoring 149 runs and allowing 127 for a 22 run margin and a .579 
pythagorean winning percentage.  Leading the league in scoring with 
176 runs were the 17-11 Rockford Files (Andrew Bernstein) who allowed
a mediocre 168 runs for the month.  Leading the league in run prevention
with 84 runs were the 18-10 St. Paul Peeps (Thom Henninger) which 
scored 124 runs and had a .685 pythagorean winning percentage.  The 
team with the best pythagorean winning percentage was the 16-12 
Northfield Nor'easters (James Bailey) who scored 148 while allowing
only 99 runs.

Bad Team of the Month:
SANFORD AND SON went 5-23, scoring 104 runs and allowing 167 runs for a 
-63 margin and a .279 pythagorean winning percentage.  All but the runs
allowed were league worsts.  The Rockford Files (Andrew Bernstein) allowed 
the most runs (168).  

Hitter of the Month:
The Rockford Files only have two of the Top 5 in runs created this month.
Leading the league was Avery Island's CARLOS MAY, with 32.9 RC and 13.3
RC27.  Rounding out the top 5 were Rockford's Harmon Killebrew (30.7), 
Avery Island's Lee May (30.7), Rockford's Mack Jones (27.3) and Sophia's
Norm Cash and Rockford's Mike Hegan (26.8).

Bad Hitter of the Month:
Ringling's Jerry McNertney created 2.8 runs for the month and 1.0 RC27 
to go with a .406 OPS in 88 plate appearances.

Starter of the Month:
Sophia's Phil Niekro saved 14.5 runs above average, going 4-0 with a 2.21 
ERA over 53.0 innings.  Runners up were Cary's Claude Osteen (14.1) and
St. Paul's Jim Palmer (13.0).

Reliever of the Month:
Hamsterdam's Ken Tatum saved 6.3 runs above average, appearing in 13 games 
and pitching 20.0 innings with a 0.90 ERA.  Runners-up were Apostles Jim Ray
(5.9) and Rockford's Hoyt Wilhelm (5.3).

Bad Pitcher of the Month:
Sanford And Son's JOE NIEKRO surrended 14.7 runs more than average over 39.0
innings with a 7.38 ERA.  Also stinking up the joint were Rockford's Jack
Aker (-13.3) and Quincy's Bill Stoneman (-12.9)
