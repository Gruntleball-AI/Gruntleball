Pitchers of the Month

Andersonville's Gaylord Perry saved 16.5 runs above average for the month of July, pitching 55 innings over 7 games with a 1.47 ERA. With 13.1 runs saved, St. Paul's Jim Palmer was more dominant on a per-inning basis but threw only 40 innings. Rounding out the field were Sanford's Tom Seaver (15.7), St. Paul's Fritz Peterson (10.7) and Hamsterdam's Larry Dierker (10.3).

The dominant relievers of the month were Sophia's Jim McAndrew, who saved 6.0 runs above average over 23 innings, followed by Hawaii's Don Shaw who saved 5.5 runs in 22.1 innings with a 1.21 ERA.

The worst pitchers of the month were Rockford's Bob Veale who cost his team 19.8 runs with 22.2 innings of 11.90 ERA ball. Also pretty awful were Hamsterdam's John Hiller (-15.5), Avery Island's Rudy May (-15.3) and Ringling's Tom Phoebus (-14.3).
