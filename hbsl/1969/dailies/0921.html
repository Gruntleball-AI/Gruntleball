In an attempt to reduce marijuana smuggling from Mexico, the Customs Department, under Commissioner Myles Ambrose, acting on the orders of President Richard Nixon, launches Operation Intercept, subjecting every vehicle crossing the Mexican border to a three-minute inspection and to many observers marking the beginning of the modern war on drugs. NY Jet Steve O'Neal punts 98 yards against Denver Broncos

This is an NFL record that stands to this day.

Here's how it happened: The New York Jets were taking on the Denver Broncos and the Jets were pinned on their own one-yard line. The Jets sent in their secret weapon, Steve O’Neal, a rookie from Texas A&M, who took the punt from his own end line. With a bit of help from the wind the ball flew 70 yards before taking a few good bounces, eventually being fielded by by the Lions Bill Thompson inside the one-yard-line.

Total length of of the longest punt in NFL history; 98 yards, breaking the 94 yard record that stood since November 16, 1931.
