wOBA Batting Leaders

For the 2nd week in a row, Andersonville'sRoy White led the league in hitting, creating 12.5 (!) runs above average with a .717 wOBA and a batting line of 594/611/1094 in 36 plate appearances. Runners-up were Apostle Hank Aaron (.763/9.3) and Ringling's Nate Colbert (.621/8.1). Bad hitter of the week goes to Rockford's Denis Menke who cost his team 5.1 runs with a .135 wOBA, closely followed by Northfield's Paul Blair who had a .091 wOBA and was 5.0 runs below average.

wOBA Pitching Leaders

C Street's Andy Messersmith pitched 15 innings with a wOBA against of .161, saving 7.8 runs above average. Runners-up were teammate Greg Garrett with a .170 wOBA and 7.0 wRSA, Quincy's Bob Moose with a .232 wOBA against and 6.0 wRSA and Apostle woodie Fryman who saved 5.9 runs with a .222 wOBA. Reliever of the week was Hamsterdam's Jim Brewer who pitched 7.2 innings and saved 5.6 runs with a .063 wOBA against. Three Sanford pitchers bottomed out the rankings, worst of which was Joe Niekro who cost his team 7.4 runs in 5.2 innings over two starts with a .579 wOBA against.
