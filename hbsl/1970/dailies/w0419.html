wOBA Batting Leaders

This season, I'm doing things a little differently for the batter of the week. Instead of using Runs Created (RC) and saving myself a lot of work, I'm using Tom Tango's wOBA (weighted on-base average) to determine who is the best hitter each week. Derived from wOBA will be runs above average, which will determine who had the best overall week.

Rockford's Joe Morgan led the league with 6.9 wRAA and a .591 wOBA, with a batting line of 462/500/808 in 30 plate appearances. Runners-up were Northfield's Paul Blair (.582/6.6), St Paul's Cesar Tovar (.624/5.7) and Cape Canaveral's Ken Henderson (.558/5.4). Bad hitter of the week goes to Cape Canaveral's Mark Belanger who cost his team 5.1 with a .100 wOBA.

wOBA Pitching Leaders

This season, I'm doing things a little differently for the pitchers of the week. Instead of using a blend of RCERA and CERA to determine RSAA (runs saved above average), to correspond with the batters, I'm using wOBA and wRAA to determine the best of the week.

Sophia's Dave McNally pitched 15 innings holding hitters to a .197 wOBA (against a league average of .337), resulting in 6.8 runs saved above average. Runners-up were Okinawa's Fergie Jenkins with a .246 wOBA and 5.4 wRAA and Hamsterdam's Vicente Romo with an amazing .118 wOBA and 5.1 wRAA in only seven innings pitched. Reliever of the week was St Paul's Dick Hall who saved 3.7 runs with a .090 wOBA. Bad pitcher of the week goes to Sophia's Don Sutton who cost his team 5.3 runs with a .472 wOBA.
