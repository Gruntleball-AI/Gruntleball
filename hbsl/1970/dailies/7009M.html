Hitters of the Month

Andersonville's Tommy Harper created 17.2 runs above average for the month, batting 371/416/629 with a wOBA of .468 in 124 plate appearances. First runner up was C Street's Rusty Staub with 15.7 wRAA and a 43 wOBA. Rounding out the field were C Street's Wes Parker with 15.0 runs above average and a .454 wOBA and Northfield's Jim Northrup who created 14.3 runs above average with a .504 wOBA. Bad hitter of the month goes to Cape Canaveral's Chris Cannizzaro who batted 1911/244/245 for the month, costing his team 12.8 runs relative to the average batter with a .205 wOBA. The next worse batter was teammate Ken Berry who cost them 12.3 runs for the month.

Pitchers of the Month

Pakenham's Sonny Siebert saved 14.2 runs for the month with a wOBA against of .238 in 47 innings. Runner up was Avery Island's Jim McGlothlin who saved 13.5 runs in eight starts with a .258 wOBA. Also strong were C Street's Wayne Simpson and Steve Hargan who saved 11.8 and 10.8 runs above average, respectively.

The dominant relievers of the month were St Paul teammatesDon Gullett and Ken Tatum who saved 6.2 and 6.1 runs above average over 13 and 12.2 innings, respectively.

The worst pitcher of the month was Sanford's Tom Griffin who cost his team 13.8 runs over 15.1 innings with a .520 wOBA against.
