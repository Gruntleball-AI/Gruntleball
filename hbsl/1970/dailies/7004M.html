Hitters of the Month

St Paul's Carl Yastremski created 24.4 runs above average with a wOBA of .602, batting 429/556/948 with 11 homeruns. Runners up were Cape Canaveral's Tony Perez with 21.8 wRAA and a .556 wOBA; Quincy's Reggie Jackson (16.3/.516); Cary's Thurman Munson (13.9/.506) and Rockford's Willie McCovey (12.5/.460). Bad hitter of the month goes to Northfield's Ray Fosse who batted 143/211/186 for the month, costing his team 11.3 runs relative to the average batter.

Pitchers of the Month

Okinawa's Ferguson Jenkins saved 16.6 runs for the month, pitching 50.1 innings over six starts, holding batters to a wOBA of .250. C Street's Steve Hargan saved 12.9 runs in five starts, holding batters to a .237 wOBA. Northfield's Bill Singer saved 11.7 runs and Hamsterdam's Joe Decker and Vicente Romo saved 11.4 runs each.

The dominant relievers of the month were Rockford's Floyd Weaver , who saved 8.3 runs above average over 17.1 innings with a .207 wOBA; teammate Hoyt Wilhelm saved 7.1 runs. Apostle Wilbur Wood saved 6.4 runs in 18 innings of relief.

The worst pitchers of the month were Northfield's Steve Carlton who cost his team 14.1 runs over 27 innings. Nearly as bad was Sanford's Reggie Cleveland who cost his team 13.8 runs.
