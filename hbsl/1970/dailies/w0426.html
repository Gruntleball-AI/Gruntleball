wOBA Batting Leaders

St Paul's Carl Yastrzemski led the league with an astounding 12.8 wRAA and a .746 wOBA, with a batting line of 577/667/1231 in 33 plate appearances. Runners-up were Quincy's Reggie Jackson (.704/10.8, which in most weeks is more than enough to win) and Cape Canaveral's Tony Perez (.678/10.0). Bad hitter of the week goes to Andersonville's Bobby Bonds who cost his team 5.8 with a .137 wOBA.

wOBA Pitching Leaders

Northfield's Bill Singer pitched 17 innings holding hitters to a .160 wOBA (against a league average of .335), resulting in an astounding 10.1 runs saved above average. Runners-up were Okinawa's Marty Pattin with a .220 wOBA and 7.0 wRAA and St Paul's Fritz Peterson with an amazing .216 wOBA against and 6.9 wRAA in 16 innings. Reliever of the week was Pakenham's Danny Frisella who saved 5.1 runs with a .155 wOBA against. Also worthy of mention is Rockford's Hoyt Wilhelm who allowed only a single in 5.2 innings pitched for a wOBA against of .047. Bad pitcher of the week goes to Okinawa's Pat Jarvis who cost his team 7.9 runs with a .598 wOBA against.
