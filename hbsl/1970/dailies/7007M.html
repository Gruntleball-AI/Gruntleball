Hitters of the Month

Rockford's Frank Howard created 21.4 runs above average for the month of July, batting 361/415/897 with a wOBA of .555 in 97 plate appearances. The first runner up was Ringling's Billy Williams with 18.4 wRAA and a .501 wOBA. Rounding out the field were Okinawa's Jake Gibbs (?) with 16.0 runs above average and a .604 wOBA; Cary's Don Money (14.4/.503) and Cape Canaveral's Tony Perez (14.4/.455). Bad hitter of the month goes to Northfield's Dick Green who really struggled batting 143/172/190 for the month, costing his team 12.7 runs relative to the average batter. Nearly as bad with -12.4 runs above average was Hamsterdam's Danny Thompson.

Pitchers of the Month

Rockford's Jim Perry saved 15.1 runs for the month, pitching 51.0 innings over six starts, holding batters to a wOBA of .247. Runners up were Cary's Bill Hands who saved 13.0 runs in six starts with a .244 wOBA; Sanford's Tom Seaver saved 12.8 runs; St Paul's Fritz Peterson saved 12.0 runs.

The dominant relievers of the month was Sophia's Tom Hall , who saved 10.1 (!) runs above average over 17.2 innings with a .169 wOBA.

The worst pitcher of the month was Rockford's Bob Veale who cost his team 15.8 runs over 23.1 innings. Nearly as bad were teammate Bob Johnson who cost his team 13.6 runs.
