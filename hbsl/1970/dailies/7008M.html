Hitters of the Month

St Paul's Carl Yastrzemski created 15.0 runs above average for the month of August, batting 346/433/644 with a wOBA of .465 in 120 plate appearances. First runner up was Cape Canaveral's Bernie Carbo with 14.5 wRAA and a .494 wOBA. Rounding out the field were Okinawa's Billy Grabarkewitz with 14.1 runs above average and a .515 wOBA; and Quincy's Al Oliver who created 12.9 runs above average with a .449 wOBA. Bad hitter of the month goes to Hamsterdam's Danny Thompson who really struggled batting 161/169/161 for the month, costing his team 14.9 runs relative to the average batter with a .141 wOBA, this following his dreadful July. The next worse batter was Quincy's Tommy Helms who cost them 9.6 runs for the month.

Pitchers of the Month

C Street's Ray Culp and St Paul's Wally Bunker each saved 14.93 runs for the month. Culp pitched more innings--51.1 to 40.0, but Bunker was more effective, with a .221 wOBA compared to .244 for Bunker. Runner up was Cape Canaveral's Bob Gibson who saved 14.6 runs in six starts with a .247 wOBA.

The dominant relievers of the month were Cape Canaveral's Dave Giusti , who saved 7.3 runs above average over 21.1 innings with a .231 wOBA and Avery Island's Diego Segui who saved 6.8 runs with a .226 wOBA.

The worst pitcher of the month was Quincy's Rich Folkers who cost his team 19.7 runs over 17.1 innings--nearly double the next worst pitcher, Sanford's Ron Herbel who cost them only 10.4 runs over 12.2 innings.
