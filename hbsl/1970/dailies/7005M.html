Hitters of the Month

Rockford's Willie McCovey obliterated the field, creating 38.6 runs above average with a wOBA of .646, batting 433/599/990 with 15 homeruns. The first runner up was Andersonville's Roy White with 18.1 wRAA and a .483 wOBA, less than half of McCovey's runs created. The remaining leaders were St Paul's Carl Yastrzemski (16.3/.512); Rockford's Ted Savage (15.0/.529), Okinawa's Ron Fairly (49.9/.478) and Hamsterdam's Sal Bando (14.9/.496). Bad hitter of the month goes to Pakenhams's Larry Bowa who batted 222/250/269 for the month, costing his team 11.8 runs relative to the average batter.

Pitchers of the Month

C Street's Steve Hargan saved 20.9 runs for the month, pitching 49.0 innings over six starts, holding batters to a wOBA of .210. Runners up were Sanford's Tom Seaver saved 16.8 runs in six starts, holding batters to a .239 wOBA; C Street's Ray Culp saved 15.0 runs; Cary's Bill Hands saved 10.4 runs and St Paul's Jim Palmer saved 10.3 runs above average.

The dominant relievers of the month were Hamsterdam's Ken Sanders , who saved 8.6 runs above average over 16.0 innings with a .168 wOBA. Rockford's Jack Aker saved 7.9 runs. Cape Canaveral's Wayne Granger and Mike Marshall saved 6.3 and 6.0 runs, respectively.

The worst pitchers of the month were Northfield's Vida Blue who cost his team 12.9 runs over 13.0 innings. Nearly as bad were Apostle Jim Ray who cost his team 11.1 runs and Sophia's Fred Norman who was 10.4 runs below average for the month.
