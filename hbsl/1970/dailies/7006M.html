Hitters of the Month

Apostle Hank Aaron created 20.1 runs for the month of June, batting 406/467/771 with a wOBA of .541 in 107 plate appearances. The first runner up was Andersonville's Roy White (again!) with 19.1 wRAA and a .500 wOBA. Rounding were Rockford's Willie McCovey (16.3/.476); Cape Canaveral's Tony Perez (15.8/.479), and C Street's Don Buford (14.4/.482). Bad hitter of the month goes to Andersonville's Mike Epstein who really struggled batting 187/264/215 for the month, costing his team 10.9 runs relative to the average batter.

Pitchers of the Month

C Street's Andy Messersmith saved 14.7 runs for the month, pitching 49.2 innings over seven starts, holding batters to a wOBA of .243. Runners up were Okinawa's Fergie Jenkins saved 14.4 runs in seven starts, holding batters to a .257 wOBA; Cary's Ken Holtzman saved 14.3 runs; Quincy's Bobby Bolin saved 14.0 runs and Sophia's Phil Niekro saved 12.2 runs above average.

The dominant relievers of the month were Sophia's Tom Hall , who saved 6.2 runs above average over 22.2 innings with a .248 wOBA and teammate Al Fitzmorris who saved 5.8 runs over 14.0 innings.

The worst pitchers of the month were Apostle Wilbur Wood who cost his team 11.0 runs over 18.0 innings of relief. Nearly as bad were Sanford's Joe Niekro who cost his team 10.5 runs and Pakenham's Rick Wise who was 10.1 runs below average for the month.
