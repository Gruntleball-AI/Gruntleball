Friday, September 27, 1975

The last use of capital punishment in Spain. A day after Spanish dictator Francisco Franco rejected worldwide pleas for mercy, Spain executed two ETA men (Txiki Paredes and Ángel Otaegui) and three Frente Revolucionario Antifascista y Patriótico (FRAP) members (Humberto Baena, José Luis Sánchez Bravo, and Ramón García Sanz) by firing squad, the last executions in Spain to date. Twelve European nations recalled their ambassadors from Spain, and thousands of people demonstrated against the government of ailing Spanish dictator Francisco Franco.

Chad's President Félix Malloum ordered all French troops to leave the Afrtican nation, because France was negotiating with Chadian rebel leader Hissène Habré for the release of French hostages. The withdrawal was completed by October 27.
