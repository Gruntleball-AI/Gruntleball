Overview

The league consists of 20 franchises replaying each year of baseball and began with the 1982 season. Seasons are played over approximately five months using Diamond Mind Baseball, currently version 12a. Standings and box scores are distributed through daily reports. Before each new season, owners submit keeper lists, the Annual Draft is conducted, and the season begins.

League Structure

The 20 franchises are divided into 4 divisions of 5 teams each. Each team plays 162 games, with the Diamond Mind computer manager managing both home and visiting teams from each owner’s submitted manager profile.

Each team plays 8 games against non-division opponents, 10 games against 2 division rivals, and 11 games against the other 2 division rivals. Division assignments are based on manager tenure: the 5 longest-tenured managers form one division, the next 5 form another, and so on. All divisions use the designated hitter.

Rosters

Teams must have sufficient batting and pitching resources to complete a 162-game schedule. A practical guide is approximately 600–700 plate appearances per position, 162 starts, and 300–350 relief appearances. Pitcher usage is measured by batters faced.

Active Roster

Of the players on a roster, 25 are active at any one time. The active roster must contain at least 24 eligible, active, and healthy players, at least 10 healthy pitchers, at least 2 healthy catchers, 2 healthy shortstops, and 2 healthy center fielders. Up to 40 players may be active during September.

Keeper Lists

A keeper list should be submitted to the commissioner by each owner within 2 weeks after completion of the World Series. Players not included on a keeper list enter the Annual Draft player pool for the following season, provided they are included in the official current-season Diamond Mind database. Different rules may apply during an expansion or vacancy draft.

Annual Draft

Draft Order

The first 3 picks of the first round are determined by a lottery among the 14 non-playoff teams. The worst team has 14 chances and the best non-playoff team has 1 chance. Picks 4–14 are assigned in reverse order of regular-season record, followed by the 6 playoff teams, also in reverse order of regular-season record. Ties are broken by run margin and then coin flip.

All subsequent rounds are drafted in reverse order of regular-season record regardless of postseason status.

Draft Picks and Roster Limits

Each team receives a number of its own Annual Draft picks equal to 45 minus the number of players on its keeper list. Picks may be traded or acquired. Each team must finish the Annual Draft with a roster of at least 32 and no more than 50 players. If a team still holds a pick when its roster has reached 50 players, that pick and any remaining picks held by the team are forfeited.

Time Allotted

The first round is conducted in a “trickle” fashion without a formal time limit. In the second round, owners have 12 hours to select, with reasonable allowances for sleep.

Beginning with the third round, owners ordinarily have 4 hours to select. A missed pick is skipped, but the owner may jump in later. A skipped owner does not receive another 4-hour window when the next turn arrives. A final skipped pick is forfeited.

Please pick quickly. Nineteen other people are waiting. Try to negotiate trades before going on the clock. When real life intrudes, owners should send a player list or selection criteria to the draft expediter and commissioner.

Annual Draft Player Pool

Only players listed in the official current-season Diamond Mind database may be drafted, regardless of whether they appeared in prior real or simulated seasons.

Manager Profiles

All games are played on the commissioner’s computer using manager profiles submitted by each franchise owner. Profile forms and instructions are provided by the commissioner. Owners may set manager tendencies and individual player tendencies. Ideally, profiles should be designed to function with minimal changes in the event of injuries.

Player Eligibility and Usage

Position and Role Eligibility

Hitters may play only positions for which they have defensive ratings, subject to these exceptions:

  • A center fielder may play any outfield position.
  • A left fielder or right fielder may play the other corner-outfield position.
  • A left fielder or right fielder rated Very Good or Excellent (B or A) may play center field.
  • A shortstop may play any infield position.
  • A second baseman rated Very Good or Excellent (B or A) may play shortstop.
  • Only rated infielders, not catchers or outfielders, may play first base.
  • Any player whose role is batter may serve as designated hitter.

Regular-Season Usage Limits

Batters may accumulate no more than 105% of their real-life plate appearances. Pitchers may face no more than 105% of their real-life batters faced.

DMB Playing-Time Limits and Hobbled Players

DMB’s playing-time-limits feature is enabled. Players perform at full strength until reaching their playing-time limit. Once a player exceeds the limit, the player becomes a replacement-level performer (“hobbled”). Owners remain responsible for monitoring usage and injuries.

The computer manager will account for a hobbled player’s replacement-level event table, although specific roles assigned in a manager profile may continue to influence how the player is used.

Exceeding Usage

A player who reaches 105% of available usage is dropped from the active roster after the game in which the limit is reached or exceeded. The roster spot remains open until the owner fills it. An overused player is not eligible again until the postseason.

Platoon Playing-Time Limits

Once a player exceeds 125% of the specified plate appearances against one side, DMB hobbles the player to replacement level against that side. The player remains unaffected against the non-overused side and may remain in the profile. Intentionally overplaying a player on one side to tank is prohibited and is enforced on the honor system.

Responsibility for Monitoring

The franchise owner is responsible for monitoring usage and injuries. Usage and injury listings accompany each daily report. Owners should read those sections and make necessary adjustments.

Teams Unable to Field a Legal Roster

A team unable to field all positions, maintain 9 healthy pitchers, 2 healthy catchers, 2 healthy shortstops, 2 healthy center fielders, or maintain a healthy 24-player active roster will be assigned restricted free agents from the available pool. The commissioner will select restricted free agents based on available PA or BFP at the needed position and the absence of a Prone injury rating.

Each restricted free agent signed results in forfeiture of a pick in the following season, beginning with the team’s own 5th-round pick. If that pick is unavailable, the next earlier available own pick is forfeited. Forfeited picks are replaced with picks at the end of the draft.

If a team has none of its own picks in rounds 1–5, its highest-WAR player in the following season is ineligible for the first 30 games. The 30 games do not count as an injury for gruntling purposes. Additional violations affect the next-highest-WAR players.

Disgruntled Players and Keeper Eligibility

A player must reach 80% of real-life playing time to be retained for the following season. A player below 80% is disgruntled, may not be kept, and enters the Annual Draft pool. A player with zero real-life PA or BFP is automatically eligible to be kept (“auto-gruntled”).

This is a firm rule. An owner’s plan to use a player later in the season does not create an exception.

In-season Transactions

During the season, owners may make trades and promote or demote players already on their rosters. Except when a team runs out of eligible players, only drafted or rostered players may appear during the season.

Disabled List

An injured player may be replaced by a reserve player only after being placed on the 15-day disabled list. The injured player remains on the disabled list for the greater of 15 days or the injury duration.

Player Movement

Frequent movement between the active roster and farm is discouraged. A farmed player must remain farmed for at least 10 days, except when an injury requires a move.

Trading

  • The trade deadline is July 31 of the season being simulated.
  • All trades must be reported by both teams to the commissioner. A trade becomes official when both teams submit updated manager profiles or adequate instructions reflecting the trade, or when the commissioner approves the trade despite one or both updates still being outstanding.
  • Draft picks may be traded only for the subsequent season’s draft. Picks from later drafts may not be traded.
  • Trades must be completed in their entirety. Performance conditions, players to be named later, and future considerations in or outside the league are prohibited.
  • A restricted free agent signed to complete a legal roster may not be traded.
  • All trades are subject to commissioner approval.

Dumping

Because this is a career-based league, dumping is completely and utterly allowed and ethical. No trades will be contested based on assumed dumping.

Qualification and Seeding

The 4 division winners and the 2 non-division winners with the best records qualify. The 2 division winners with the best records receive first-round byes. The remaining division winners are seeded 3 and 4, and the wild cards are seeded 5 and 6. The highest remaining seed plays the lowest remaining seed. Ties are broken by head-to-head record, overall run differential, and coin flip, in that order.

Postseason Rosters and Series Format

The 25-player postseason roster may include as few as 9 pitchers. Rosters may be changed at the start of each series. All 3 rounds are best-of-seven in a 2-3-2 format, with the higher seed hosting games 1, 2, 6, and 7.

Postseason Usage

In each series, batters are limited to 10% of real-life plate appearances. Pitchers are limited to 10% of real-life games and games started. Once a player exceeds the series limit, the player is removed from the 25-player roster for the remainder of that series.

Postseason Injuries

An injured player may be replaced. If replaced, the injured player is ineligible for the remainder of that series and the following series, if applicable.

Postseason Broadcasts

Postseason games, and occasional regular-season games, may be broadcast live from the commissioner’s computer using a screen-sharing service. Attendance and accompanying banter are encouraged.

Awards

At the conclusion of the World Series, each owner is expected to congratulate the league champion by email and copy the commissioner.

Managers vote for MVP, Cy Young, Fireman, and Manager of the Year. Each team votes for 10 MVP candidates, 5 Cy Young candidates, 3 relief pitchers for Fireman, and 3 managers other than the voter for Manager of the Year.

Award voting is mandatory. The commissioner will set a deadline approximately one week after the final World Series game.

Penalties

The commissioner intends penalties to be unusual. Owners who experience circumstances that prevent ordinary participation should notify the commissioner. Reasonable accommodation will be made when the commissioner is informed; extended radio silence is not acceptable.

Missing the Initial Profile Deadline

An owner who misses the initial profile deadline forfeits the team’s own 5th-round pick in the next draft. The commissioner assigns the active 25-player roster.

Not Fielding a Legal Roster

A regular-season roster must contain 24 healthy players, including at least 9 healthy pitchers, 2 healthy catchers, 2 healthy shortstops, and 2 healthy center fielders. A first violation costs the team’s own 5th-round pick. Acquired picks may not substitute. Later violations cost progressively earlier own picks. Lost picks are replaced with picks at the end of the draft.

If no own pick remains in rounds 1–5, the team’s highest-WAR player the following season is ineligible for the first 30 games. Additional violations affect the next-highest-WAR players. Those 30 games do not count as injury time for gruntling.

Missing the Awards Ballot Deadline

The owner forfeits the team’s own 5th-round pick in the following draft. If unavailable, the next earlier own pick is forfeited through the 1st round. If none remain, the highest-WAR player is unavailable for the first 30 games of the following season.

Missing the Keeper-List Deadline

The commissioner assigns the keeper list: all gruntled and auto-gruntled players, regardless of quality, less the gruntled batter with the most upcoming-season PA and the gruntled pitcher with the most upcoming-season BFP.

Amendments

Any league member may suggest changes, additions, or deletions. A non-binding vote may be taken to gauge support and encourage discussion. The final decision rests with the commissioner. This is a benevolent dictatorship, not a democracy.

League Administration

League Files and Website

The commissioner will identify the official location of the league database, constitution, statistics, daily reports, and other league resources.

Waiting List

The league does not currently maintain a waiting list. League members are welcome to recommend suitable candidates when an opening occurs.

League Officers

The commissioner administers league operations and may consult experienced owners or appoint owners to advisory or draft-administration roles. Current officeholders should be listed in the Albumball Directory rather than permanently embedded in the constitution.
